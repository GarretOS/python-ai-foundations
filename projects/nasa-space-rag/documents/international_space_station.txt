Title: International Space Station Research and Technology
Source: NASA — Space Station Research and Technology
Source URL: https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/space-station-research-and-technology/

The International Space Station (ISS) is an orbiting laboratory that has supported continuous human presence for more than twenty years. It provides a place for scientific investigations and technology demonstrations that use the space environment. The station’s research covers many areas, including astrophysics, combustion, microbiology, and life science.

Scientists conduct research aboard the ISS because its microgravity environment makes it possible to study questions and test technologies in ways that are not possible on Earth. The station is described by NASA as a platform for cutting-edge science that is unlocking discoveries unavailable in an Earth-based laboratory. Researchers can also explore projects involving how materials and liquids behave in microgravity, as well as biological investigations.

The work can benefit people on Earth and support future exploration. NASA uses the station to test technologies important to returning to the Moon and to pursue medical and social breakthroughs. Research results include investigations into space-caused bone loss, which may help doctors protect astronauts on long missions and inform treatments for bone conditions on Earth. The ISS National Laboratory also makes the space-based research platform available to U.S. researchers from companies, universities and other institutions, government agencies, and other organizations.
